Our Mission

The Global Orchard Initiative Network is a community-based organization in Busia District focusing on orchards, afforestation, environmental conservation, and agriculture-based empowerment.

Objectives

  • Modern fruit farming and sustainable environmental practices
  • Urban and rural orchard promotion
  • Food security and nutrition awareness
  • Horticulture opportunities for women and youth
  • Community education and agri-business hubs
  • Nursery management and fruit tree propagation

Established: 2008

Partners: Busia Forest, Natural Resource, Health & Education Departments
