Our Mission

Global Orchard Initiative Network is a community-based organization operating in Busia District in the areas of orchard farms, afforestation, environmental conservation, and modern agriculture practices. We empower women through savings, horticulture, and food security programs.

Objectives

  • To provide modern agricultural and environmental practices through fruit growing and orchards
  • To promote orchards in urban and rural communities
  • To promote food security and nutrition
  • To promote horticulture among women and youth dropouts
  • To open up information, education, and agriculture business centers
  • To promote nursery management and fruit growing

Year Established: 2008

Annual Cash Turnover: Less than $500,000

Network Participation: Busia District Forest, Natural Resource, Health & Education Departments

Active Projects

  • 🌱 Planting Orchard Farms in Public Institutions
  • 📚 Workshops for Schools about Environmental Conservation
  • 👩‍🌾 Women Empowerment through Savings Culture
  • 🍎 Training Communities on Food & Nutrition
  • 🌿 Horticulture for Women & Youth Dropouts
