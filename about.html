Our story

Rooted in community.

Orchid Initiative works with people in Busia District to grow fruit trees, share skills, and care for the places they call home.

Why we work

Our work brings orchard farming, environmental care, and practical learning together. We believe communities should be at the centre of decisions about their land and livelihoods.

How we work

We collaborate with schools, local leaders, women, young people, and other community members on planting and training activities. Projects are shaped by the needs of the people involved.

What matters to us

Care for the environment, useful education, and opportunities for wider participation guide the work. We want every project to be practical and meaningful to its community.
