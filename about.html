🌸 About Orchid Initiative

Our roots run deep in community and conservation.

Our Beginning

Founded in 2008 in Busia District, Uganda, Orchid Initiative began with a simple mission: use the power of plants and education to transform lives. What started as a small orchard project is now a flourishing network supporting food security, reforestation, and women’s empowerment.

Our Vision

We believe that nature and community thrive together. Our goal is to build resilient ecosystems, reduce poverty, and create green jobs—especially for youth and women in rural areas.

Our Team

Led by passionate volunteers and agricultural experts, our team includes educators, nursery technicians, and district community officers who work side-by-side with local leaders.

Why Orchids?

Orchids symbolize beauty, diversity, and endurance—values we want to cultivate through every farm, classroom, and family we reach.
