👩‍🌾 Women Empowerment & Healthcare Development

Building healthier communities through women-led initiatives and sustainable practices.

The Women Empowerment & Healthcare Development Project is dedicated to supporting women in Busia District through agriculture, community healthcare, and entrepreneurship. By integrating orchard farming with health education, we aim to improve both livelihoods and wellness outcomes for families.

Our teams collaborate with healthcare professionals and local leaders to create platforms where women can access knowledge, tools, and training in nutrition, environmental care, and small-scale agribusiness — ensuring long-term community resilience.

Project Impact

  • 👩‍⚕️ 300+ women trained in health and agricultural sustainability
  • 🏥 10 community health and nutrition outreach programs conducted
  • 🌿 Increased household income through women-led fruit farming
