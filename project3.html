Women and community wellbeing

Connecting agricultural learning with nutrition, health awareness, and opportunities for women.

This initiative brings orchard farming together with community conversations about nutrition, wellbeing, and livelihoods. It creates opportunities for women and families to take part in practical learning.

Orchid Initiative works with local leaders and community members to make these activities useful to the people involved.
