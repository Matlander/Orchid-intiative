🌱 Orchard Farms in Public Institutions

Planting the seeds of sustainability across schools and communities.

The Orchard Farms Project is a core initiative of the Orchid Initiative designed to establish orchards in schools, hospitals, and local government compounds across Busia District. Through this, we aim to promote food security, nutrition, and environmental awareness.

Working hand-in-hand with Busia District authorities, this project trains youth and women in sustainable agriculture, orchard management, and fruit tree propagation.
