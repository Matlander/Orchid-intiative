Community planting

Community orchard expansion

Growing fruit trees alongside communities and sharing the skills needed to care for them.

Community orchard work brings local people together around planting, tree care, and shared learning. The aim is to support access to fruit while strengthening local knowledge and environmental care.

Activities include hands-on planting and conversations about maintaining young trees as they grow.
